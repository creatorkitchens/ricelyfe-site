Wait — is this inside an arcade?

The window’s on Shotwell. Walk up, no arcade required.

We run the kitchen out of Joey the Cat Arcade at 3252 19th Street. The street-facing window opens directly onto Shotwell — order there and you don’t have to go inside. Or come in, play a game, eat at the arcade window. Up to you.

Right up the block from Hedge Coffee, if you know the neighborhood.
